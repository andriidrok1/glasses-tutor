# Glasses Tutor privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Glasses Tutor is an iPhone app that takes a photo with your Ray-Ban Meta glasses when you press the capture button, sends it to an AI provider and reads the answer back through the glasses.

## What the app sends and where

When you press the capture button, the photo is sent from your iPhone directly to the AI provider you picked in the app (OpenAI or Anthropic), together with your own API key and up to three earlier photos from the last 20 minutes. Nothing is sent before you turn on "I agree" in the app's setup. The provider's own privacy policy applies to that request:

- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Glasses Tutor has no server of its own. The developer does not receive your photos, answers, API key or any usage data. The app turns off the Meta Wearables SDK analytics and crash reporting.

## What stays on your phone

- Your API key, in the iPhone Keychain, on this device only.
- The last 20 photos, in the app's Documents folder (visible in the Files app). Deleting the app deletes them.
- Settings such as language and speech rate.

## Camera and audio

The glasses camera is used only while the app takes a photo you asked for with the button. The capture LED on the glasses is on while it does. The app does not record audio or video.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact and deletion

Everything the app keeps is on your phone, so deleting the app removes it. Requests to the AI provider are handled under your account with that provider. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/andriidrok1/glasses-tutor/issues.
